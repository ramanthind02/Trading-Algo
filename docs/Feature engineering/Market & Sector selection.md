## Stocks 

- Long only systems tend to outperform due to risk-premia obtaining from holding stocks.  
- Tend to **mean revert in short-term** and **trend over medium-to-long term**.
- High liquidity, moderate volatility.
- **Long-only mean reversion** works well (buy-the-dip behavior).

# Energy 
- Strong **macro and seasonal trends**, often driven by supply/demand shocks, geopolitics, and inventory data.
- **High volatility**, prone to **regime changes** (sideways vs explosive trends).
- Often **momentum-dominant**, especially during crisis or supply shocks.

# Commodities
- **Trend-following dominates** due to long-term supply/demand imbalances.
- Often show **long persistent trends** but can **mean revert sharply** after exhaustion.
- Many are **seasonal**, especially agricultural ones.
- **Momentum + spread trading** (calendar spreads) work well.

# Metals 
- **Precious Metals (Gold, Silver, Platinum, Palladium)**
	- **Gold:** Mean reverting vs real yields; **momentum** works in crises.
	- **Silver:** More volatile, good for **momentum breakouts**.
	- Often **anti-risk assets**, **inverse correlation** to USD/real yields.
- **Base Metals (Copper, Aluminum, Nickel, Zinc)**
	- Strong **macro & industrial demand** drivers.
	- **Momentum and trend-following** perform well during global expansions.

# Forex
- **Relative value markets** → mean reversion dominates unless strong macro divergence.
- **Carry trade** (interest rate differential) matters for long-term strategies.
- Major pairs are more liquid and efficient, so **edges are small**.
- Very high liquidity, major pairs are very efficient markets 
- **Major Pairs (EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, etc.)**
    - **Range-bound / mean reverting** most of the time.
    - **Momentum** works during central bank divergence or crises.
    - **Volatility breakout** trades succeed on macro regime changes.
- **Minor / Cross Pairs (EUR/GBP, GBP/JPY, etc.)**
    - Less liquid → more **mean reverting** behavior.
    - Prone to **fake breakouts** and **sharp reversals**.

# Bonds / Fixed income 
- **Trending during macro regime shifts** (inflation/deflation cycles).
- **Mean reverting** in stable policy environments.
- **Low volatility**, highly liquid for majors.
- **Mean reverting** short-term, **momentum** during Fed policy cycles.
